KLDTS Backup

KLDTS Backup is an internal data backup utility operated by KLD Trading Solutions, LLC. It uses rclone to copy designated local files to an authorized Google Drive account for backup and recovery.

Purpose and access

The utility is intended for use by its authorized operator only. It is not a public service and does not offer public registration or access to backup files.

Google authorization allows the utility to maintain its designated backup files without requiring the operator to sign in for every scheduled run.

Privacy

For information about Google account access, data handling, retention, and revoking access, see our Privacy Policy.
