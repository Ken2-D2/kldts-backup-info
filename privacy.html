Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

This policy describes how KLDTS Backup, an internal utility operated by KLD Trading Solutions, LLC, handles information in connection with Google Drive.

Information accessed

The utility uses Google OAuth authorization and the Google Drive API with the drive.file permission scope. It accesses files and folders created by or specifically authorized for the application, including their contents and metadata (such as names, identifiers, sizes, and modification times). It copies designated local backup files to the operator's authorized Google Drive account. OAuth credentials and tokens are maintained in the local rclone configuration.

How information is used

Google Drive information is used to authenticate backup operations, locate backup folders, compare files, transfer backups, and support backup verification and recovery. Local operational logs may record filenames, transfer results, and errors for troubleshooting. Google user data is not used for advertising, sale, profiling, or training generalized AI or machine learning models.

Sharing and storage

The utility transfers backup data to the authorized Google Drive account using Google's API. It is intended for internal use and does not sell Google user data or disclose it to unrelated third parties. Google processes and stores the uploaded information under its own terms and privacy policy. The operator controls the local backup configuration, access to backup files, and credentials. This informational website does not host backup files or include application analytics, advertising, or forms; its hosting provider may process ordinary web request information.

Retention and deletion

Backups are retained in the operator's Google Drive account until removed by the operator or applicable retention settings. Local configuration and logs are retained as needed for backup operations. The operator can delete backup files through Google Drive and remove local credentials and logs. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously uploaded files.

Authorization and control

The operator can revoke the application's access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized backup operations until access is granted again.

Google API data use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to KLD Trading Solutions, LLC through the support email displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
